About SDRL

A simple link between transport demand and vehicle supply.

SDRL operates as a transport broker. We receive transport requirements from companies and coordinate with cargo owners and vehicle partners to arrange suitable road transportation.

Our focus

  • Understanding company transport requirements
  • Coordinating suitable vehicle availability
  • Connecting companies with cargo and vehicle partners
  • Supporting smooth booking and movement coordination
